# Calibration notes (2026-10-02)

The scripts are in `.git/kaine-tools/analysis/self-rhythm-calibration/` and will be added to this change with the final validation report.

## The spiking-unit generator could not produce the rhythm

The first implementation put recurrence and depression directly on the 16 snnTorch LIF units, stepped at 20 Hz (`sweep1.py`, `sweep2.py`, `inspect1.py`).

- Across 108 configurations of recurrence, depression rate, bias gain and recovery time, plus 1,458 configurations that added per-unit noise and an excitability floor, **none** produced an undriven 0.5–0.9 Hz rhythm at resting, mid and alert own drive.
- The units either fell silent (no spontaneous activity to ignite recurrence) or fired tonically. At most about 5 of 16 units fired in a step (p90 rate 0.31), so the population never produced the population-wide episodes the mechanism relies on. Its low-frequency "peaks" were filtered noise (peak fraction 0.2–0.3).
- Cause: a 16-unit population, stepped every 50 ms with a roughly 0.5 s membrane, is too coarse and too noisy for recurrence-plus-depression episodes.

## The mean-field generator works

Tabak et al. (2000) model this mechanism as a mean-field rate population (`meanfield1.py`, `meanfield2.py`, `proto_r.py`):

    tau_a da/dt = -a + 1/(1 + exp(-(w·s·a + margin + own_gain·own + gain·ext + noise)/k))
    ds/dt       = (1 - s)/tau_r - U·s·a

It is integrated at 20 Hz with 10 substeps. The working regime: w = 2.0, k = 0.1, tau_a = 0.05 s, U = 4, margin = -0.3 (excitability below the recurrence threshold), own_gain = 0.05, noise 0.02.

- **Undriven rhythm.** Frequency falls monotonically with tau_r: 1.27 Hz at 0.8 s, 1.18 at 1.0, 0.82 at 1.2, 0.73 at 1.6, 0.62 at 2.0, 0.55 at 2.5. Below about 0.8 s the oscillation breaks down, so plasticity is clamped to tau_r in [0.9, 3.0]. The ceiling of about 1.27 Hz means a maternal rate above about 76 bpm cannot be matched 1:1.
- **Own drive.** A ±0.03 shift in margin (own drive across rest to alert, with own_gain 0.05) moves the frequency by up to about 0.38 Hz. This is the arousal confound (V6).
- **Afferent gain, no plasticity (3 h).** PLV against the beat: 0.02–0.04 at gain 0.05, about 0.05 at 0.1, 0.10–0.15 at 0.2, 0.28–0.43 at 0.4. **Gain 0.1 is outside the Arnold tongue:** the beat alone does not capture the rhythm.
- **Plasticity sign.** Sign -1 is the one that works: tau_r moves from 1.6 toward about 1.0 and the rhythm locks to the beat (PLV 0.98). Sign +1 drives tau_r to its 3.0 s limit, where the generator becomes merely excitable and fires on each beat pulse: an evoked response with PLV about 0.9. The withdrawal frequency-pull criterion rejects that, because without the beat the system falls back to about 0.5 Hz. Too-fast plasticity (eta 5) crashes tau_r to its floor and kills the oscillation.
- **Specificity (eta 1, 4 h).** Driven frequency follows each maternal rate: 0.95 / 1.07 / 1.17 / 1.27 Hz for 57 / 64 / 70 / 76 bpm, PLV 0.94–0.98. Final tau_r: 1.08 / 1.06 / 1.03 / 0.95.
- **Foreign mother** (exposed to one mother, scored against another): 0.01–0.27, below the surrogates. Fails, as required.
- **No plasticity:** PLV 0.05 throughout. Fails, as required.
- **Withdrawn frequency** (20 s drive-off every 30 min, eta 1, 3 h):
  - No plasticity: a steady 0.73 Hz.
  - With plasticity: pulled toward each rate. Means over six withdrawals are about 1.02 / 1.15 / 1.19 / 1.18 Hz for 57 / 64 / 70 / 76 bpm. The order holds at the lower rates and compresses at the ceiling.
  - A single 20 s estimate is noisy (about ±0.1 Hz), so specificity is judged over several withdrawals.
- **Arousal varying at rest** (own drawn around mid-rest every second): the lock holds (PLV 0.72 against a surrogate maximum of 0.21), but the withdrawn frequency wanders between 0.58 and 1.05 Hz.
- **Surrogates.** Foreign mothers at the same mean rate drift apart slowly, so a single 300 s window can show a high surrogate PLV (up to about 0.8). A real lock still exceeds it, but the margin varies.

## Consequences for the design

- The rhythm is generated by the Tabak mean-field population. The 16 LIF units are driven by its activity, so the oscillatory-binding layer still gets a spiking population and a Hilbert phase. This faithfully implements the cited model, and section 2 of the design is updated to match.
- The plasticity sign is fixed at -1 by calibration. The beat frequency still appears nowhere in the code.
- eta is being calibrated over 48 h runs so that the median first lock falls at 12–72 h. At eta 0.2–1, locking takes 0.5–2 h.

## Learning rate and arousal coupling (48 h and 24 h runs; `eta48b`, `og0.02`, `og0.01`)

- The first 48 h batch used a plasticity floor of 0.3 s, below the class's 0.9 s clamp, and drove "seed 1" from one mother while scoring it against another. Corrected: clamp [0.9, 3.0] as in the class, and each run is scored against its own mother. The miswired seed became a free foreign-mother control; it never passed.
- **Time to first earned lock** (arousal varying at rest, own_gain 0.05, 70 bpm; the two seeds agreed): about 30 h at eta 0.005, 15 h at 0.01, 7.5 h at 0.02.
- **Lock quality, hours 24-48.** The PLV beat all 19 surrogates in 100% of 5-minute windows for every true condition (57, 70, 76 bpm). The foreign-mother control beat them in 5% of windows: the p < 0.05 false-positive rate the test is built for.
- **The arousal confound is real at own_gain 0.05.** With arousal fluctuating second by second, the withdrawn frequency reached only about 0.87 Hz against a 1.17 Hz beat (pull about 0.3), and tau_rec pinned at its floor. At constant arousal, pull was about 1.
- **The fix is weaker arousal coupling, not a looser criterion.** Results at own_gain 0.02, eta 0.02, arousal at rest, over 24 h:

| Condition | First lock | PLV beats surrogates (12-24 h) | Pull |
|---|---|---|---|
| 57 bpm | 5.7 h | 100% | 0.68 |
| 70 bpm | 9.2 h | 100% | 0.88 |
| 70 bpm, seed 1 | 10.3 h | 100% | 0.92 |
| 76 bpm | 14.0 h | 100% | 0.65 |
| No plasticity | never | 100% (weak evoked phase relation) | 0.01 → fails |
| Foreign mother | n/a | 2% → fails | — |

  - At own_gain 0.01, 76 bpm became unreachable (withdrawn frequency 0.74 Hz).
  - **The no-plasticity row shows the frequency-pull test is load-bearing.** The surrogate test alone detects even a weak evoked phase relation. Pull is what separates entrainment from an evoked response.
- **Chosen:** own_gain 0.02 (class default) and eta 0.01 (`[soma].self_rhythm_eta`). That eta targets a median first lock of about 20 h, inside the 12-72 h window. Full validation (V1-V6, 96 h) is running.

## Generator retune and final settings (candidate A)

- **The 96 h validation of the first tuning failed V2.** The marker passed in only 0-2% of withdrawals at 70 bpm, because self-sustain failed: the learned tau_r sat at about 0.9 s, the fragile edge of that generator's oscillation, where its intrinsic amplitude falls about fourfold (0.31 at 0.73 Hz to 0.085 at 1.1 Hz).
- **`sweep3.py` and `charA.py` found candidate A:** w 2.5, U 8, margin -0.25, tau_a 0.02, k 0.08.
  - It oscillates from about 1.6 Hz (tau_r 0.9 s) down to about 0.75-0.9 Hz (2.2 s), with amplitude 0.17-0.29 throughout, so the maternal range (1.0-1.33 Hz) sits mid-band.
  - It stops oscillating above about 2.4 s, so tau_r starts at 2.1 s and is clamped to [0.9, 2.3] s.
- **Afferent gain.** Candidate A is easier to capture: at gain 0.1 the beat alone captured it, with no plasticity (median PLV 0.66). That is forcing, which the design forbids. At gain 0.03 the drive alone does not capture it (median PLV 0.10, and 0.24 at full drive).
- **Learning rate.** At gain 0.03, eta 0.01-0.02 locked too fast (2-6 h); eta 0.0025 gives about 14 h at 70 bpm.
- **Final settings:** candidate A, afferent_gain 0.03, own_gain 0.02, eta 0.0025, plus replication over 3 withdrawals. The results are in `validation.md`.
