## Why

The tenth test boot wrote about 1.7 GB of research event log a day, and nothing removed it: `retention_days = 0` keeps research records forever, so a long run fills the disk the entity runs on. The paper (predictive-workspace-paper #49 and #50, section 3.5 and A.5 "Housekeeping" and "Awake floor", Table A1) makes this part of sleep. Every sleep, last before the affective reset, reclaims regenerable or spent data oldest first until the bytes on each resource (disk, host memory) of such data and unexported study records are within a per-resource budget `B^hk_j`, or until `Delta_hk` has passed. Regenerable or spent data is what no module reads again and the entity's continuation does not need: process and container logs, caches rebuilt on demand, bus entries acknowledged by every consumer group and older than the restart replay window, and local study-record copies whose export to the archive volume a checksum has verified. Unexported study records are never deleted. An excess left over carries over, and awake fatigue builds at `nu_act (1 + P^hk)`. Awake, a floor outside the entity's state keeps each data volume's free space above a reserve `R^hk`. Neither path touches the memory stores, the world model, the self-model and identity, voice adapters or preservations.

## What Changes

- New `kaine/modules/sleep/housekeeping.py`. It accounts the files under the study-record roots (`data/evaluation`, which holds the research event log and the evaluation sinks, and `data/workspace_trajectory`, the broadcast log) and the rotated backups of the process log. While over `disk_budget_bytes` and within `time_allowance_s`, it walks them oldest first: a log backup is deleted, and a closed daily study-record file is exported to the operator's `archive_dir` (copied, synced, checked by SHA-256, renamed) before the local copy is removed. A local copy whose archive copy already matches is removed; one whose archive copy differs is kept. With no archive, study records stay. Host memory is the bus's memory use against `memory_budget_bytes`. The report carries the three terms and the pressure `P^hk`.
- The allowlist: housekeeping acts only on regular files inside the study-record roots and on the current process-log backups, and refuses symlinks, paths that resolve elsewhere and anything under `state/`. The archive may not lie inside a root or under `state/`.
- The sleep module runs housekeeping at the end of the pipeline, before the affective reset, adds the report to `sleep.sleep.completed` under `housekeeping`, and hands its terms to a process-wide pressure holder (`kaine/housekeeping_pressure.py`). An aborted sleep skips it.
- Fatigue: the interoception module's accumulator builds at `e (1 + P^hk)` while awake; asleep and with no excess it is unchanged.
- The awake floor (`kaine/cycle/housekeeping_floor.py`), started by the cycle: every `floor_period_s` while awake it checks the `data` and `state` volumes against `R^hk = max(reserve_bytes, reserve_share x volume size)`. Below it, it reclaims regenerable data (never exporting) until free space reaches `2 R^hk`; still below `R^hk`, it suspends the study record (`kaine/persistence/study_record_gate.py`; study-record sinks write a gap marker at the start and the dropped count at the end) until free space is back at `2 R^hk`. Each action is logged and published on `housekeeping.out`, and the disk's excess at that moment counts in `P^hk` until the next sleep.
- Exports keep every byte and need a mounted archive: only records dated before yesterday are exported, the source is re-checked through its copy descriptor before removal (a change repeats the export once, then keeps the file), a stale prefix copy is replaced, a file that vanishes, becomes a link or has several hard links is skipped without stopping the pass, and an archive that is not a mount point or another filesystem exports nothing. Roots and watched volumes come from the configured study-sink directories; sinks under `state/` are watched but never reclaimed. The floor reclaims only on the low volume, aims at `min(2 R^hk, (size + R^hk) / 2)` so the record can always resume, and fails boot on bad values. Boot and the dashboard warn when no archive is mounted.
- New `[sleep.housekeeping]` table with operator budgets and conservative defaults. The research event log's `retention_days = 0` stays; its files leave the host only through the archive.

## Not in this change

The paper's definition covers data this code base does not hold, so nothing is reclaimed for these: container logs (kept and capped by the container runtime), caches rebuilt on demand (none in the data tree), and bus entries (the bus uses no consumer groups and has no restart replay window). Host memory is therefore not measured either: the paper counts only acknowledged bus entries older than the replay window, the bus has neither, and the memory term is 0. The pressure lives in memory, so after a restart it is 0 until the next sleep or floor action measures it.

## Capabilities

### Modified Capabilities

- `sleep`: every sleep runs housekeeping last before the affective reset, and an awake floor keeps free space above a reserve.
- `interoception`: awake fatigue builds at `nu_act (1 + P^hk)`.
