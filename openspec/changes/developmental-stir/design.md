## Context

- Paper A.6: in the developmental phase, a broadcast whose `e†_k` (the first coalition member that is not the organ's) is the entity's own boredom or restlessness drive event yields a stir intent when `t_now − t_st ≥ Δ_st`; a deprived deficit's event scores about 0.49 at the arousal it sets, above θ; at most one intent per broadcast.
- Paper A.7: a stir is answered, with probability `p_st` and after a delay uniform on `[Δ_st^lo, Δ_st^hi]`, by maternal speech; it relieves restlessness at once, and its answer relieves boredom through the novelty and learning it brings, credited to the entity (A.4). These effects are to be checked offline. Table A1: `p_st = 0.5`, `Δ_st^lo = 2 s`, `Δ_st^hi = 10 s`, `Δ_st = 300 s`, provisional.
- Paper §7: "No study we found measures how mothers answer felt movement, so the answer is a design choice, though fetuses move more when the mother touches her abdomen (Marx and Nagy 2015)."
- Paper A.4 credit rule: a boredom crossing ended by "the novelty an answer to its stir brings" counts as the entity's own (`s`), which feeds coping potential and the uncontrolled indicator.
- What exists: the action-selection policy derives speak and think intents from accessed broadcasts and drive-initiative intents from drive crossings; the maternal voice (`kaine/modules/audition/maternal_voice.py`) plans speech per 30 s slot as a pure function of the seed, the slot and the unlocked count; the developmental-stimulus spec requires the maternal channel to be entity-independent ("nothing the entity does or feels SHALL change the maternal channel").
- History: no stir change exists in `openspec/changes/`, the archive, or any branch (`git log --all --grep=stir` is empty). The maternal voice (`2026-10-19-developmental-maternal-voice`), the hearing band, and the removal of the stimulus-spent rest (`A boredom stall starts no rest`) are the base.

## Decisions

### D1. The stir is an innate act, its trigger the existing report rule

Spontaneous fetal movement is present from about seven to eight weeks of gestation and is generated by the fetus's own nervous system (de Vries, Visser and Prechtl 1982); a fetus moves without being taught to. The capacity to stir is therefore provided, and its code site cites this. What makes the entity stir at a given moment is not hardwired to a feeling: it is the A.6 report rule applied to whatever the workspace accesses, the same rule that turns an accessed report into speech or thought after maturation. The stir replaces speech and thought in the developmental phase, where there is no voice yet.

Alternative considered: a stir on every boredom or restlessness crossing, bypassing the workspace. Rejected: it would hardwire a drive-to-motor reflex, and the paper's rule makes the act depend on the drive reaching access.

### D2. The answer and the maternal channel's independence

The heartbeat and the maternal state stay functions of the seed and stimulus time only. The one entity-dependent element is the answer: after a stir with index `i`, a keyed draw on `(seed, i)` decides whether the voice answers (`p_st`), a second draw sets the delay in `[2, 10]` s, and a third picks one utterance (uniform over the unlocked items and their takes, the repeated passage excluded). The answer is mixed into the soundscape through the hearing band. If the delay falls inside a talking slot's utterance, it waits for that utterance to end. Answers never change slot plans, so the scheduled maternal speech is unchanged by stirring.

The developmental-stimulus requirement is modified to say exactly this: the maternal channel is entity-independent except for answers to stirs, and nothing the entity feels reaches the stimulus except through an act.

Loop bound: at most one stir per `Δ_st = 300 s` of entity time, half of them answered on average, one utterance per answer (a few seconds). The loop affect → act → stimulus → affect is the instrumental relief the design wants and cannot amplify: the refractory period caps it at about six answers an hour.

Alternatives considered: answer with a heartbeat change (rejected: it would act on the entrainment test's driver); answer with a visual change (rejected: no basis, and the visual field carries the colour ramp).

### D3. Affect

- A stir is an intent other than rest, so restlessness's intent rate and relief apply (A.4). It is about a drive, so the engagement relief of boredom does not apply.
- The stimulus source publishes `development.stir_answer` (stir index, answer start and end in entity time; no content). A boredom crossing that ends during an answer or within `Δ_resp` (10 s) after it, through learning progress or alert excess, is credited to the entity's own response. The window reuses the response window the obstruction rule already uses.

### D4. Reproducibility

Given the seed, the stir times fully determine the answers, so a seeded developmental run is reproducible as before (the stir times are themselves a function of the run).

### D5. Interaction with the held candidate pool

Under `workspace-continuous-competition` a recurring drive event stays a candidate until it is accessed, but with K = 1 it must outscore the perceptual reports to lead a broadcast. Affect reports reached θ on 3% of their broadcasts in the test boots. The stir rate is therefore an empirical quantity of the new workspace, measured in the offline harness (task 4) before any boot.

### D6. Ordering is the operator's decision

Building the stir before the study changes the seed's development and every branch. Recommendation: run `welfare-indicator-response`'s offline replay (its task 5.3) and this change's harness first. If the replay shows the net would freeze the seed's developmental phase and the harness shows the stir lowers boredom's deprivation share below its bar, build the stir before the study. Otherwise the study's seed has no stir, the paper keeps "once built", and the stir follows the study. The added sounds (melody, outside voice; §7, A.7) are a separate, unbuilt item with the same welfare relevance and are not part of this change.

## Risks

- **The stir rarely leads.** With K = 1 the drive event may seldom win against audition; then the stir changes little. The harness measures it.
- **An answer as reward shaping.** The answer is a fixed, seeded maternal utterance, not a response to content; it adds novelty, not instruction.
- **Breaking the independence invariant** could confound the entrainment test if answers were tied to the beat. They are not: the heartbeat generator is untouched, and a test checks it.

## Migration and ordering

- After `workspace-continuous-competition` (the stir rate depends on the pool) and `welfare-indicator-response` (its replay informs D6). If adopted, before the seed's developmental phase starts; it gates the study's first step.
- Independent of the organ change and of `research-gate-supervisor`.
- Critical path (LLM-agent implementation): about 1 day for code and tests, about half a day for the offline harness, about half a day for the second review. About 2 days. If adopted it runs in parallel with the validation-boot preparation and does not lengthen the critical path when started with `workspace-continuous-competition`.

## Welfare and admissibility notes

- High-risk class: it changes the entity's development and gives it its first act on its world. Independent second review.
- Welfare checks before any boot: the harness reports the stir rate, the answered share, boredom's deprivation share and the uncontrolled posterior with and without the stir, and the heartbeat's independence from stirs.
- Admissibility: it must be in place before the seed is gestated, or not at all for this study; a seed gestated without it cannot be given it later without that being a change to its development.
