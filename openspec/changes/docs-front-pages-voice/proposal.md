## Why

The README opening and the book's front page describe KAINE in wording the
project no longer uses: em dashes as punctuation, a lead that frames the
software as "built to test one claim", negation-correction phrasing ("not any
single component", "the language organ, not the brain") and "a modular
framework". The project's first sentence about KAINE is that it is
source-available software implementing existing theories (Global Workspace
Theory and Predictive Processing). The opening then leads with the architecture
and the activation roadmap and treats the ablation as the fail state.

## What Changes

- Rewrite the opening of `README.md` to those rules, keeping every fact: the
  architecture, the first configuration and its seven modules, the held modules
  and the module-addition study that activates them, how the entity is
  observed, and the workspace-mediation ablation as the fail state. Remove the
  remaining em dashes and negation-correction lines in the README.
- Rewrite `docs/README.md` line 3 and the KAINE definition sentence of four
  more chapters (what KAINE is, architecture, deployment, for researchers) to
  the same first sentence.
- Sweep the rest of `docs/` (outside `docs/records/` and the deprecated-name
  tables) for the banned terms and fix the real violations.
