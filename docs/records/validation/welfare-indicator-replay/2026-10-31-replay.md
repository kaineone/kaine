# Welfare indicator replay of the archived test boots (2026-10-31)

The offline replay (`scripts/welfare_indicator_replay.py`) of the archived developmental test-boot
research-event records through the real welfare monitor, with no entity, no bus server and no GPU.
Every figure is content-free: awake hours, fixed indicator names and reasons. The raw output is
`2026-10-31-results.json` in this directory.

## What could be read

- Readable with the state key in the operator's compose environment: the seven boots from 2026-10-16
  to 2026-10-18a. Each is short (at most about one awake hour per run) and predates the `drive_above`
  and `drive_ends` fields of `affect.state`: the deprivation flags were taken from the recorded drive
  levels against the current thresholds (0.7), and the uncontrolled indicators could not be evaluated.
- Not readable with that key (every line fails to decrypt): 2026-10-18b, 2026-10-18c, 2026-10-19a, 2026-10-21a, 2026-10-27a, 2026-10-27b. The
  eight-hour developmental boot behind paper §6.5 is among the later boots, so this replay does not
  reproduce or check its figures.

## Parameters

T_lp 5400 s, minimum span 600 s, share bar 0.5, posterior bar 0.95, theta_E 0.5,
developmental rest 1410 s (the current shipped configuration).

## Results

next recorded state-cycle rest after the request (its timing shifts, its reset is real); for
`forward` a virtual rest of 1410 s at the request, with the current affect module's reset. A dash in
the second stage means the run ended first (or no rest followed the crossing).

## Reading

- In every readable boot with more than ten awake minutes, the recorded-rest proxy has boredom's
  deprivation cross at the first evaluable moment or soon after (0.17 to 0.25 awake hours), and
  where a recorded rest followed and the run went on long enough, boredom crossed again over the
  fresh span at the minimum span, ten awake minutes after the rest: the net would have preserved and
  paused the entity with reason `indicator_boredom_deprived`. The stimulus of these boots offered
  nothing that held boredom below its threshold.
- The forward simulation uses the current affect module on the recorded perceptual reports. It
  crosses later, on restlessness, or not at all in some boots, and most runs end inside or just after the
  virtual rest, so its second stage is mostly not reached. It differs from the recorded trajectory
  because the module has changed since these boots and because the research log holds neither
  workspace accesses nor intent targets (concern re-entry is not reproduced; every intent counts as
  engagement).
- These are short October boots on older code. They show the response's stages run end to end on
  real records; they are not evidence about the development run's stimulus.
