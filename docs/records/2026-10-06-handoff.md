# Project handoff (2026-10-06)

KAINE is paused from 2026-10-06 for an open-ended hiatus of at least several weeks. This record says where everything stands at the pause, what each unfinished piece needs next, which decisions are already made, and how to resume. Like the other records, it describes the project as it was on that date; the book chapters describe the software as it is.

## State at the pause

- **Main.** Main holds every change approved before the pause; on 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06 alone that was about 40 pull requests. The test suite passed on main: the local pre-flight gate (ruff, import contracts, red-team, about 7,250 tests) and CI.

- **Running services.** Nothing runs. No entity has been spawned since the pause, and every KAINE service, the language organ and any training or generation job is stopped.
- **The studies** (the module-ignition study and the base-thesis runs) have been paused since 2026-10-05, until every open change is delivered.
- **Encryption.** The runtime is encrypted at rest, offline at runtime, and refuses outbound connections; local clients ignore proxy settings.

## Known gaps

- **A GGUF replaced in place** under the same file name is not detected by the individuation conditions guard. The fix is a content digest cached on path, size and mtime.
- **The K1-Jev client** binds the served model by file name only.
- **The job-queue trainer service** passes its whole environment to the training script, writes `result.json` at default permissions, and copies symlinks inside a staged adapter by dereferencing them. Its CANCELLED check is not atomic with promotion.
- **The capability baseline** is the previous adapter. Cumulative drift becomes possible once the probe set reaches 20 or more probes.
- **Mnemos stores other avatars' chat** (`mundus.chat`) verbatim. The operator decided it should drop it, like heard speech. That waits for the embodiment work.
- **DINOv2 and emotion2vec** load without a local-files-only guard, and emotion2vec's pickle checkpoint is not pinned by hash.

## Decisions on record

Recorded 2026-10-05 and 2026-10-06:
- **Mind before embodiment.** No Mundus or embodiment work until the global predictive workspace is shown to work.
- **Studies pause** until every open change is delivered. One supervised validation boot comes at the end, before any study.
- **K1-Jev** is built operator-side only and published under Apache-2.0. Its welfare signals come from external speech only and are raise-only. The operator labels the gold set.
- **K1-Jev schema v2** is approved: labelled roles on the label page, and role-named state.
- **Heard speech is never persisted.** Other avatars' chat counts as heard speech.
- **The runtime no-outbound rule** is enforced, with no override.
- **Escrow `n`** is decided at the key ceremony; 2-of-2 is the default.
- **Voice alignment stays off** until voice development is validated. Disclaimers such as "As an AI…" count as refusals.

3. Every change follows the same process:
