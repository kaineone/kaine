# Episodic recall content: preregistration note

Status: requirement recorded; pilot equivalence bounds not yet finalized.
Source: `openspec/changes/episodic-recall-content/design.md` (Welfare and admissibility) and task 7.3.

## Requirement

For any study run with episodic recall content on, each run must report,
against the content-off arm:

- recalls (total),
- recalls with content,
- recalls accessed (content-free),
- the welfare observer's negative-valence share, and
- open drive crossings.

The comparison of negative-valence share and open drive crossings between
content on and content off requires equivalence bounds fixed in the study's
preregistration before its pilots. No equivalence bounds are fixed yet.

## Planned bounds (TBD)

The numeric equivalence bounds for the welfare observer's negative-valence
share and open drive crossings (content on versus content off) remain TBD.
They are planned for the preregistration but are not invented here. They
must be finalized before any live pilot with recall content on.

Note: the entity-free loop-check bound (at most 10 percentage points excess
negative-trace share during the episode; arousal returns to the off-case level
within the neutral period) applies only to the preregistration-exempt
entity-free loop check and is not the pilot equivalence bound.

## Next action

Before scheduling pilots with recall content on, finalize and record in the
study's preregistration the equivalence bounds for negative-valence share and
open drive crossings, then mark task 7.3 complete.
